About Fire & Vine

Fire & Vine is a relaxed grill and wine restaurant at Athlone Circle shopping centre in Pietermaritzburg. We cook with fresh, preservative-free ingredients from local farms and suppliers, and welcome families, friends and colleagues with friendly service.
